Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 13, 2026

Welcome to Imperial Matrimony ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. If you have any questions or concerns about this privacy notice or our practices regarding your personal information, please contact us.

This Privacy Policy applies to all personal data collected through our mobile application Imperial Matrimony, related services, sales, marketing, or events.

1. Information We Collect

To deliver a personalized, secure, and reliable matchmaking experience, we collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when registering on the app, creating or updating a profile, or communicating with other users.

  • Personal & Identity Details: Full name, gender, date of birth, marital status, photograph(s), government-issued ID (for account verification purposes).
  • Contact Details: Email address, phone number, physical address/location.
  • Profile Preferences & Demographic Info: Community, religion, mother tongue, education, profession, lifestyle preferences, partner expectations, height, and age preferences.
  • Communication Data: Messages, text contents, and media sent via our secure real-time in-app chat feature.
  • Device & Usage Information: Device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers, IP address, app log data, and crash reports.

2. How We Use Your Information

We process your information for purposes based on legitimate business interests, fulfilling our service contract with you, compliance with legal obligations, and your consent. Specifically, we use your data to:

  • Facilitate account creation, profile verification, and login processes.
  • Provide intelligent matchmaking recommendations based on your preferences.
  • Enable direct, secure real-time messaging between shortlisted candidates.
  • Verify user authenticity to maintain a safe, 100% verified matrimonial community.
  • Respond to user inquiries, customer support requests, and address technical issues.
  • Prevent fraudulent activity, spam, harassment, and security incidents.
  • Enforce our Terms of Service and comply with applicable legal regulations.

3. How Your Information is Shared

Your privacy and confidentiality are our highest priorities. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

  • Public/Member Profile Sharing: Information you choose to display on your matrimonial profile (such as age, community, profession, bio) will be visible to registered users seeking matches.
  • Control Over Private Details: Contact details, full real name, and restricted photos are shared strictly based on your individual privacy settings and mutual consent.
  • Service Providers: We may share data with trusted third-party service providers who perform services for us (e.g., SMS/Email gateways, cloud hosting, real-time database hosting, customer support tools). These third parties are bound by strict confidentiality agreements.
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose information if legally required to do so by court order, law enforcement, or regulatory authority.

4. Data Retention and Account Deletion

We retain your personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this privacy policy, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law.

Account Deletion & Right to be Forgotten: You can request account deletion at any time directly through the app settings or by contacting our support team. Upon deletion, your profile, messages, and associated personal data will be permanently removed from our active database, subject to legally required records retention.

5. Security of Your Data

We implement appropriate technical, administrative, and organizational security measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. All in-app chat messaging and data transmissions are encrypted using standard industry encryption protocols.

6. App Permissions

To provide core features, the Imperial Matrimony app may request access to the following permissions on your mobile device:

  • Camera & Photos/Media: Required to upload profile pictures and send images via in-app chat.
  • Notifications: Required to send real-time alerts for incoming messages, match interests, and updates.
  • Internet Access: Required to synchronize profile data, preferences, and real-time messaging.

7. Children's Privacy

Our app is intended exclusively for legal adults seeking matrimonial relationships. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal data from anyone under the age of 18. If we learn that we have collected personal data from a minor without verification of parental consent, we will delete that information immediately.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. The updated version will be indicated by a revised "Last Updated" date at the top of this page.
